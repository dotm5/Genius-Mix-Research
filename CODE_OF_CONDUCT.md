# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission;
- publishing device databases, library contents or account identifiers of other people;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards, and take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful: a private warning, a temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban.

Report abuse through GitHub: contact the maintainer ([@dotm5](https://github.com/dotm5)) or use the repository's private reporting channel. Complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter are respected.
